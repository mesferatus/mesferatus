## 🖤 About Me

Hello, I'm **Callisto**. I'm 19, studying **Systems Analysis**, and building my path between **backend development, data, automation, and cybersecurity**.

I like technology when it becomes practical: APIs that behave well, automations that remove repetitive work, dashboards that make decisions clearer, and security analysis that helps understand what a system is really doing. A lot of what I know came from building, breaking, fixing, documenting, and trying again until the system finally made sense.

I have worked with **Python automation, data analysis, dashboard development, and Salesforce-related business processes**. During my time at **Eve Air Mobility**, I worked close to internal data and operational workflows, which gave me a stronger view of how technology supports real decisions.

Right now, I am especially focused on **Java/Spring backend systems**, **Blue Team cybersecurity**, **networking fundamentals**, and **turning messy processes into structured tools**.
